# Email Scam Shield - Complete Overview

Welcome to the **Email Scam Shield** overview. This document provides a high-level summary of the application, its purpose, its target audience, and the core features that make it an effective tool against phishing and email scams.

## 🎯 Purpose & Value Proposition
**Email Scam Shield** is a mobile application designed to educate and protect users against the growing threat of email scams and phishing attacks. By combining real-time scanning tools, localized threat intelligence (specific to India), and gamified learning, the app transforms cybersecurity awareness from a passive chore into an engaging, proactive habit.

The core philosophy revolves around:
1. **Protection**: Actively scanning user inputs (emails/domains) for known phishing vectors.
2. **Awareness**: Providing localized data on how scams operate in different regions.
3. **Education**: Using gamification to condition users to spot red flags effortlessly.

## 📱 App Structure & Flow
The application utilizes a sleek, cyberpunk-themed 5-panel swipeable dashboard.

### 1. Protection Dashboard
The entry point. It provides a real-time status of the user's protection settings, a quick toggle for notification security, and high-level statistics on the user's scan history and threat encounters.

### 2. Manual Email Scanner
The core utility tool. 
- Users can paste email content, sender addresses, or URLs.
- The AI-driven logic cross-references inputs against known phishing keywords, lookalike domains (e.g., *Arnazon* instead of *Amazon*), and urgency triggers.
- An animated risk meter immediately classifies the input as **Safe**, **Suspicious**, or **High Risk**.

### 3. Scam Alerts Map (India Focus)
A localized threat intelligence center.
- Users can select from 30 Indian states to view region-specific scam trends (e.g., KYC frauds in Maharashtra, lottery scams in West Bengal).
- Interactive flip-cards reveal exactly how these local scams operate and how to prevent them.

### 4. Cyber Runner Educational Game
A gamified learning experience designed to build reflex recognition of scams.
- Players select an avatar and progress through 10 increasingly difficult levels.
- At each step, they are presented with a scenario or "gate message" and must quickly decide if it is a safe communication or a scam attempt.
- Scoring determines the player's rank (from *Needs Training* to *Cyber Defender*).

### 5. Profile & Achievements
The progression tracker.
- Tracks lifetime emails scanned, scams detected, and game scores.
- Users unlock 8 distinct achievement badges as they utilize different parts of the app, encouraging continued engagement and comprehensive learning.

## 🌍 Localization & Accessibility
Recognizing the diverse user base, the app goes beyond a single language. It features robust dynamic localization, currently supporting:
- **English** (Default)
- **Telugu**
- Architecture supports rapid onboarding of additional languages and RTL (Right-to-Left) layouts.

## 🎨 Aesthetic & Design Language
The app embraces a **Cyberpunk / Advanced Security** aesthetic.
- **Color Palette**: Deep navy backgrounds (`#0F172A`), electric blue (`#3B82F6`), cyan accents (`#22D3EE`), and purple glows (`#A855F7`).
- **UI Elements**: Glassmorphism effects, neon borders, pulsing alerts, and fluid state transitions to make the experience feel both premium and highly technical without being overwhelming.
